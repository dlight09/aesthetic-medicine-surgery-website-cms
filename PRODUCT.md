# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

French-speaking prospective patients in Tunisia and abroad who are researching plastic, aesthetic, reconstructive, hand surgery, or aesthetic medicine with Dr Sirine Soussi. They arrive with personal, often sensitive questions and need a calm way to understand indications, risks, limits, consultation logistics, and how to make contact by phone or WhatsApp.

The secondary user is the practice team maintaining intervention pages, blog content, bookings, and before/after cases through the CMS.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Dr Sirine Soussi's medical expertise, practice philosophy, interventions, patient information, and appointment paths for a French medical audience. Success means visitors quickly understand the scope of care, feel informed rather than pressured, trust the safety and rigor of the practice, and can contact the cabinet without friction.

For the CMS, success means the team can publish medically careful, SEO-ready content without breaking the public site's credibility or compliance posture.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, premium.

The voice should feel expert and reassuring: clinically grounded, attentive to nuance, and confident without spectacle. The brand may reference artistry, sculpture, proportion, and natural results, but always through the lens of surgical judgment and patient safety.

## Anti-references

Avoid hype, exaggerated beauty claims, guaranteed outcomes, aggressive sales language, before/after sensationalism, and luxury-clinic excess that makes care feel performative. Avoid sterile hospital coldness, generic wellness softness, and any visual or written pattern that suggests aesthetics are being sold as a promise rather than discussed as a medical decision.

## Design Principles

- Earn trust before asking for action.
- Make medical limits as visible as benefits.
- Let precision carry the premium feeling.
- Keep contact paths immediate but never pushy.
- Treat content, SEO, compliance, and design as one patient-information system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for public and CMS surfaces. Maintain strong text contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation and dialogs, clear focus states, reduced-motion support, descriptive alt text for clinical imagery, and careful before/after consent/disclaimer treatment. Copy should remain understandable for non-specialists while preserving medical accuracy.
